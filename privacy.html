Privacy Policy

JournalGrade is a Chrome extension that adds journal indicators to Google Scholar publication entries. This notice describes v0.4.4 with the opt-in gate in this release candidate.

Information processed

On Scholar search and profile pages, it processes visible publication titles, venue details, citation text and available DOI values to identify journals, display local indicators and provide update checks. Profile summaries count rows currently loaded. It does not request Google credentials or read full manuscripts.

External services

Only after “Enable JournalGrade,” a publication title may be sent to OpenAlex when local and visible data are insufficient to identify a venue. When a DOI is available, it may be sent to Crossref for metadata and update notices. Requests go directly from the extension to those services, whose own practices apply. “Not now” stops processing on Scholar pages.

Storage and controls

Bundled/imported ranking rows and lookup caches are stored in Chrome extension storage. Withdraw consent in extension Ranking Data settings. Clear publication and DOI lookup caches there, delete imported ranking data, or remove the extension.

Limitations

Ranking coverage differs by source and edition. Missing indicators do not mean a journal is unranked or unindexed. DOI-based notice screening depends on available identifiers and upstream metadata; no alert does not prove there is no notice. Verify with original sources.
