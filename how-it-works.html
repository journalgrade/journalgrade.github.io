How it works

From Scholar entry to journal context

  1. Install from the approved Chrome Web Store listing.
  2. On first use, review the disclosure and choose whether to enable it.
  3. JournalGrade reads visible metadata on Scholar pages and matches bundled records.
  4. If enabled, uncertain venue resolution may send a title to OpenAlex; DOI checks may send a DOI to Crossref.
  5. Review source labels and verify important decisions at the source.

Withdraw consent in extension settings. “Not now” stops Scholar page processing.
