About

Research context, close to the literature

JournalGrade makes journal identity, ranking context and publication-update metadata easier to inspect while using Google Scholar.

Indicators are contextual information, not a measure of an individual article or researcher. JournalGrade is independent and is not endorsed by Google Scholar or named data providers.
