Data sources and coverage

What the bundled data covers

  • ABDC 2025: 2,648 rated rows from the supplied workbook.
  • ABS/AJG 2024: 1,822 supplied rows.
  • FT50: 50 titles labeled 2026.
  • SCImago SJR 2025: 31,753 score records; 31,076 include a best quartile.
  • Web of Science: 12,906 selected SSCI/ESCI records; complete MJL, SCIE and AHCI coverage is not established.
  • Scopus: 30,305 Active records in the blended seed. CiteScore quartile and UTD24 positive records: none present in the seed at audit time.
  • Update notices rely on DOI metadata in Crossref and retraction signals in OpenAlex.

Completeness, licensing and redistribution permissions are not independently verified. Missing indicators do not establish non-indexing. Verify records with providers.

Sources: ABDC, Chartered ABS, FT, SCImago, Clarivate, OpenAlex, Crossref.
